Chess Clock Voice

Privacy policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Short version: Chess Clock Voice does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any data. It reads the clock text on chess.com, speaks the remaining time locally, and stores your settings in Chrome sync storage.

1. What the extension does

Chess Clock Voice is a Chrome extension that speaks the remaining seconds on a chess.com clock when time gets low. It is a clock-only accessibility and time-management tool. It does not read the board, evaluate positions, suggest moves, or interact with chess.com's game logic.

2. Data we collect

None. We do not collect, use, or transfer:

  • personal information, names, email addresses, or account identifiers;
  • chess.com account data, game history, results, ratings, or opponents;
  • board positions, moves, chat, messages, or any game content;
  • health, financial, authentication, location, or web-history data;
  • analytics, telemetry, crash reports, or advertising identifiers.

The extension has no server, no user accounts, no analytics SDK, and no remote code. No data leaves your browser.

3. What the extension reads on chess.com

On chess.com live game pages, the content script reads only the clock text (for example 0:09.3 or 3:00), which clock is currently running, the time-control label (for example 3|2), and an orientation attribute used to keep clock-side handling explicit. This information is processed in memory to decide what to speak. It is never stored, transmitted, or logged to a remote service.

4. Settings storage

Your preferences (thresholds, toggles, voice settings, phrase, and custom presets) are stored using chrome.storage.sync. This keeps them available to the extension and, if you have Chrome Sync enabled, lets Chrome sync them across your signed-in browsers. That sync is a Chrome platform feature; the developer has no access to it. Settings contain no game data or personal information.

5. Speech synthesis

Announcements use the browser and operating system's built-in speech synthesis. The spoken text is generated locally. The extension does not send audio or text to a cloud speech service.

6. Permissions

storage saves your settings and presets.
https://*.chess.com/* lets the declared content script read the clock on chess.com game pages. No other hosts are used.

7. Third parties and sharing

We do not sell, rent, transfer, or disclose data to third parties. There are no advertising, analytics, or tracking partners. The extension is distributed through the Chrome Web Store, which is operated by Google and governed by Google's own policies.

8. Children

The extension is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone, including children.

9. Fair play

The extension is not a chess engine, does not analyze positions, and does not provide move suggestions. It only speaks clock time. Users are responsible for following chess.com's terms and fair-play rules.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this URL with a new "last updated" date. Material changes will be reflected in the extension's store listing.

11. Contact

For privacy questions, use the developer support contact shown on the Chess Clock Voice Chrome Web Store listing.

Chess Clock Voice is an independent extension and is not affiliated with chess.com.
